### ✨ What's New (User-Friendly)
- **Universal Tracker Editor & Reliable Sync:** Open the **Trackers** studio on any entry and fill out your status, progress, 0.1-step rating slider, dates, and notes once in the default **Universal** view — OmniTrack automatically adapts scores and pushes to all your connected services in parallel. Clearing a rating, lowering Bangumi episode counts, or refreshing expired logins now syncs cleanly across AniList, MyAnimeList, Bangumi, Trakt, SIMKL, TMDb, and VNDB.
- **Season Ratings & Personal Notes:** Long-press any season poster or chip to rate individual seasons with a smooth 0.1 slider, `+`/`−` steppers, quick score adjustments, and personal season notes. Rated seasons display a star score badge and note icon right on your season strip.
- **Combine Cross-Medium Editions:** Link a work across formats (for example, Anime + Manga + Light Novel + Book + Game) using the new **Editions & Adaptations** switcher or from **Entry Options → Link / Combine Edition**, letting you jump between mediums in one tap.
- **Where to Watch with Service Logos & Auto-Country Detection:** Movie, Show, and Anime Details pages now show official rounded-square streaming service logos grouped by Stream, Free/Ads, Rent, and Buy. Your country is detected automatically from your device by default, with a searchable country picker to browse availability anywhere in the world.
- **Awards & Honors + Award Winners Browser:** Details pages now display clean frameless Award & Honor rows (Oscars, Emmys, Golden Globes, BAFTA, Crunchyroll Anime Awards, and more powered by Wikidata and OMDb). Tap any award to browse other winning works and add them straight to your library.
- **Discover Overhaul (Fully Customizable):** Added **Daily Critics' Pick**, **Browse by Streaming Service** (with official logos synced to your region), **Top 10 Today** numbered posters, **Because You Liked** taste-matched picks, **Curated Spotlight Lists** (Under 90 Min, Hidden Gems, Limited Series, 2020s–90s), and a swipe-card **Discovery Queue** in the header. Every new section can be toggled on or off in **Settings → Discover Layout & Sections**, and all new Details sections can be reordered or hidden in **Settings → Details Page Layout**.
- **Instant Airing Schedule + Month Calendar Grid:** **Up Next & Airing** now loads immediately on startup from a local cache while refreshing in the background, and **Release Calendar** adds a **14-Day | Month Grid** switcher with month navigation and release dot markers.
- **Library Health Repair & 1-File Full Backup:** Added a **Repair** tab inside **Settings → Backup, Restore & Repair** to check database health, clear orphaned rows, and clean broken file links while leaving all custom entries and custom artwork untouched, plus a clear **Select All (1-File Full)** preset.
- **Discord & Telegram Release Alerts:** Added **Settings → Release Alerts (Discord / Telegram)** to send optional release pings to your Discord channel or Telegram chat whenever a tracked episode, chapter, movie, or game in your library drops (with a **Send Test Ping** button).
- **Navigation & Home Progress Fixes:** Fixed pressing Back from **Categories & Media Types** when opened from Library Filters (now returns to Filters), ensured **Wide Split Continue Tracking** cards always display the progress bar and percentage, and fixed exiting Details returning cleanly to your exact Library category tab (such as Web Novels).
