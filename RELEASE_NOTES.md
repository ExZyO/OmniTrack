### ✨ What's New (User-Friendly)
- **Seasons Studio — Merge, Reorder & Customize Seasons:** Combine sequels, movies, OVAs, and spinoffs right into a title's **Seasons** section from either your library or online search—complete with real episode lists. Open **Manage Seasons** (or the three-dot menu) to drag seasons into any order, rename seasons, pick custom season posters (from your gallery, preset artwork, or a URL), hide/show seasons, unlink merged entries, accept one-tap **Merge All** suggestions, keep progress synced per entry/tracker, or jump straight to a merged season's own page.
- **Accurate Multi-Season Anime Splitting:** Multi-season anime whose episodes were previously collapsed into fewer seasons (such as *Bungo Stray Dogs* showing 3 seasons instead of 5) now detect official season and cour structures properly.
- **Reliable Episode Ratings for Shows & Anime:** Fixed misleading 6.0–6.5 ratings appearing next to an IMDb badge on TV and anime episodes. Episodes now fetch authentic IMDb ratings when an OMDb key is set, fall back to verified Trakt ratings by default, filter out low-vote outliers, and only show the IMDb logo for genuine IMDb scores.
- **Fixed “Group Anime Seasons” in Library:** Turning on **Group Anime Seasons** now cleanly folds both merged seasons and multi-season anime entries into a single Library card with combined episode totals, while keeping entries separate whenever the toggle is off.
- **Refreshed Editions & Adaptations Cover Cards + Header Switcher:** Replaced tiny boxed cover tiles with larger, frameless poster cards that match the Seasons row, and added a quick layout switcher button right in the **Editions & Adaptations** header alongside long-press custom labels.
- **Reliable Back Navigation on Linked Titles & Awards:** Pressing **Back** after opening a linked edition, an award winner that was already in your library, or a merged season now returns smoothly to the previous title page instead of exiting to your Library.
- **Clean Artwork for Visual Novel Banners + Custom Art Everywhere:** Automatic Visual Novel banners now skip gameplay screenshots and choose clean promotional art instead. Whenever you manually choose a cover or banner for any title, that choice overrides everywhere—including the Home Hero, Discover Hero, Daily Critics' Pick, and browsing shelves.
- **Per-Category Daily Critics' Pick in Discover:** Switching categories in **Discover** (All, Anime, Manga, Novels, Visual Novels, Movies, Shows, Games, Books, Comics) now displays a dedicated **Daily Critics' Pick** tailored to that category.
- **Faster, Smoother Library Tab Swiping:** Eliminated frame stutters when swiping horizontally across Library tabs by moving heavy grouping calculations out of frame draws and keeping nearby tabs warm.
