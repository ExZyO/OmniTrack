### ✨ What's New (User-Friendly)
- **Discover Highlights Right on Home (Toggle & Reorder Anytime):** **Because You Liked**, **Top 10 Today**, and **Daily Critics' Pick** now appear right beneath your personal tracking rows on Home out of the box. You can also turn on the **Discovery Queue** launcher card, **Browse by Streaming Service**, and **Curated Spotlight Lists** inside **Customize Home**—and drag or hide any of the 6 rows freely while keeping Discover for full search and deep browsing.
- **Smooth Discovery Queue Swiping + Undo:** Fixed left-swipe stalls and jerky card drags in **Discovery Queue**. Cards now glide smoothly left or right with spring snap-back if released early, scale the next card up from underneath as you drag, stamp *Plan to Watch* or *Skip* badges dynamically, and include an **Undo** button to bring back your previous choice.
- **Top Theme-Colored “+ Add to Library” on Previews:** When previewing a title you haven't added yet, **+ Add to Library** now appears right beneath the header (above the synopsis) in your active theme accent color instead of hiding at the bottom.
- **Editions & Adaptations — Smart Labels + 3 Switcher Styles:** Linked seasons, spinoffs, or sequels in the same category no longer show duplicate labels (e.g., multiple repeated *ANIME* tabs). Choose your favorite switcher style in **Settings → Details Page Layout** via miniature visual previews—**Smart Labels** (with long-press custom short labels), **Cover Cards**, or **Grouped Dropdown** (e.g., *Anime (3)*).
- **Per-Medium Details Page Layout Settings:** Split **Details Page Layout** into category tabs so each media type only shows sections that actually apply to it (for example, chapters for Manga/Fanfiction and playtime for Games/Visual Novels), with fluid drag reordering that never conflicts with tab swipes.
- **Award Winners “In Library” Recognition:** Browsing award winners from **Awards & Honors** now checks your library automatically and marks existing entries with **✓ In Library** (tap to open your library page directly).
- **Universal Tracker Editor, Season Ratings, Where to Watch & Alerts:** Also includes the unified **Universal** tracker editor, per-season 0.1-step ratings and notes, region-aware **Where to Watch** service logos, instant airing schedule caching with a **Month Grid**, **Library Health Repair**, and **Discord / Telegram Release Alerts**.
